PICSONDE
Picometer OFI sensing, from laser to chip

An embedded displacement and vibration sensor based on optical feedback interferometry (OFI), combining a photonic integrated circuit (PIC) — capturing the FM channel of the OFI signal — with a system-on-chip (SoC) for real-time signal processing, targeting quantum-limited resolution for experimental modal analysis and predictive maintenance.

01The concept

In optical feedback interferometry, a fraction of the light backscattered by a remote target re-enters the laser cavity. The laser diode becomes a self-aligned micro-interferometer: source, interferometer and detector in a single package. PICSONDE pushed this principle onto a photonic chip.

Four technological barriers, solved together

The project identified and addressed the four locks preventing OFI sensors from reaching quantum-limited performance on real, speckle-affected targets:

1. High dynamic-range OFI acquisition — exploiting the FM channel, limited by the laser linewidth instead of shot noise, through on-chip FM-to-AM conversion.

2. Non-linear OFI signal retrieval — modelling OFI as an inherent non-uniform sampling (NUS) system, directly processed by the SoC.

3. Real-time assessment of the feedback factor C — direct and AI-based estimators, plus active control with an integrated variable optical attenuator (VOA).

4. Fringe detection under speckle — deep-network fringe detection robust on unseen experimental signals.

02Key results

PICSONDE delivered the first photonic-integrated OFI displacement sensor exploiting the FM channel, validated experimentally on speckle-affected targets and driven in real time by an embedded SoC.

Photonic integration

FM-to-AM conversion on a SiN chip

Integrated Mach–Zehnder interferometers and micro-ring resonators demonstrated as in-chip frequency discriminators for the OFI FM channel, achieving 1 pm/√Hz noise PSD above 5 kHz — published in Journal of Lightwave Technology (2024), with integrated electro-thermo-optic modulation of the SiN filters characterized in Photonics (2026).

Feedback control

Toward active control of the feedback factor C

A PIC-integrated variable optical attenuator provides robust OFI operation under variable feedback, rather than merely compensating C in software — published in Optics Express (2025).

Signal processing

Non-uniform sampling beyond dynamic-range limits

The OFI signal is treated as an inherent event-based, non-uniform sampling system, lifting the displacement dynamic-range constraint of closed-loop approaches — with zero-crossing NUS integration in IEEE Sensors Journal (2023).

AI × OFI

Deep networks for fringe detection & C estimation

Augmentation-assisted deep networks achieve robust fringe detection on unseen experimental signals; neural estimators retrieve C across feedback regimes — IEEE TIM (2023, 2025), Sensors (2022), Applied Optics (2025).

Open science

Open dataset

Experimental speckle-affected OFI signals with reference C values released on Zenodo, enabling reproducible benchmarking of fringe-detection and C-estimation algorithms.

Industrial validation

Embedded prototype & in-situ tests

The laser + PIC were succesfully assembled via a fiber array. The signal processing is not yet embedded in an SoC. Next step will consist in developping real-time algorithm on SoC and finally to evaluate the complete system toward real-time experimental modal analysis, with end-user ACOEM for predictive maintenance scenarios.
